About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for writing down your ideas fast. It keeps all your notes in one place so you can find them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes page.
  2. Type your note in the text box.
  3. Click Save to keep your note.

Features

  • Fast note writing
  • Simple and clean design
  • Easy to search your notes

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + S Save note
Ctrl + N New note
